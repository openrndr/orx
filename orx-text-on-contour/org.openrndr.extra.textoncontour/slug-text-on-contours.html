slugTextOnContours

fun slugTextOnContours(drawer: Drawer, boundaryDrawer: BoundaryDrawer, glyphMap: BoundaryGlyphMap, baseStyle: TextStyle, spans: List<TextSpan>, contours: List<RectifiedContour>, startT: Double = 0.0, baselineOffset: Double = 0.0)(source)

Draws spans along contours using the Knuth-Plass line breaking algorithm, with boundaryDrawer, for example a org.openrndr.draw.slug.SlugDrawer or the boundary drawer of a Drawer. The boundaries of the glyphs are kept in glyphMap.

Every contour acts as a single line, the length of the contour is used as the line width. Text that does not fit on a contour overflows to the next contour, text that does not fit on any of the contours is discarded.

The vertical alignment of baseStyle determines the contours that are used when the text does not need all of them: the text is moved to later contours by the vertical alignment times the largest number of contours it can be moved without less text fitting, rounded to a whole contour. With a vertical alignment of 0 the text starts at the first contour, with 1 it ends at the last contour.

Parameters

baseStyle

the style that is cascaded into the style of every span, layout properties (alignment, justification, direction) are taken from this style

contours

the contours on which the text is placed, in order

startT

the normalized position on every contour at which the text starts

baselineOffset

the distance between the contour and the baseline of the text, positive distances move the text towards its ascenders. Glyph spacing and line widths are measured on the offset baseline.
