NoiseWarp

class NoiseWarp : WarpPreset(source)

Displaces coordinates by fractal simplex noise, with octaves octaves that each scale by lacunarity and are weighted by decay. The noise is sampled on a circle of radius in the third dimension, phase moves along it, which loops for phases that differ by 2.
