History

class History(source)

The edits of a control manager that can be undone and redone: changes of the values of controls by the user, of the keyframes of timelines, of the order of lists and of the layouts of docks. The commands Undo (mod+z) and Redo (mod+shift+z) go through it. A history that is not enabled records nothing, like when the program has a history of its own.
