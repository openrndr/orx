class FontManager

History
Link copied to clipboard
class History

The edits of a control manager that can be undone and redone: changes of the values of controls by the user, of the keyframes of timelines, of the order of lists and of the layouts of docks. The commands Undo (mod+z) and Redo (mod+shift+z) go through it. A history that is not enabled records nothing, like when the program has a history of its own.

Inspector
Link copied to clipboard
class Inspector

The inspector of a control manager, like the inspector of the developer tools of browsers: it shows the element under the pointer with its margin, border, padding and content, and a panel with its path, its box, the style sheets that select it in the order of the cascade, with the declarations that other declarations override, and its computed style. The panel is shown in a window of its own, see inWindow. F12 turns it on and off, shift-F12 pins the element, so that the pointer can move away from it.
