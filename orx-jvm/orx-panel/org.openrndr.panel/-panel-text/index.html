PanelText

class PanelText(source)

Text drawn from the outlines of its glyphs with the boundary drawer of the program, Windfoil by default, instead of from textures of glyphs. Text is crisp at any scale and in any transform. The work is cached at every step:

  • faces are loaded once per font, weight and size, by FontFaces, shared with compositions; text is shaped in one face per font and weight and its glyphs are scaled to the size of the text

  • text is shaped once per face and string, kept for the most recently used maxShapedTexts strings; the shaped text gives both the measures that layout uses and the glyphs that are drawn, so they agree

  • the outline of every glyph is uploaded to the GPU once, for all sizes, in the glyph map of the boundary drawer that all elements, compositions and other text drawers share, see GlyphMaps

  • the draw commands of every text an element draws are prepared once in a buffer of their own, in the coordinates of the element; drawing them again, also when the element moved because it scrolled, only draws the buffer. Buffers that were not drawn in a redraw are released after it, see releaseUnused
