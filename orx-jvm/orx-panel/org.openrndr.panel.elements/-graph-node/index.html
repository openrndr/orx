GraphNode

class GraphNode(val title: String, val inputs: List<Port> = emptyList(), val outputs: List<Port> = emptyList(), var position: Vector2 = Vector2.ZERO, val data: Any? = null)(source)

a node of a NodeEditor: its title, its inputs at the left and outputs at the right, at position; under the ports the body that the host draws, and under that the status

bodyPointer
Link copied to clipboard
var bodyPointer: (NodeBodyEvent) -> Boolean?

handles the pointer in the body, like for a toggle or a slider drawn there: called with a press in the body, true takes the press, after which the drags and the release go here too, and the node is neither selected nor moved; false lets the press select and move the node. The editor redraws after every call. Changes made here can be undone by recording them in org.openrndr.panel.editHistory of the editor.

true when the node is drawn compact: the title and the rows of the ports that are connected only, without its body and its status, whose color still tints the title; null follows NodeEditor.compactNodes.
