MathLayout

class MathLayout(val origin: Vector2, val width: Double, val ascent: Double, val descent: Double, val glyphs: List<TypesetGlyph>, val rules: List<Rectangle>)

A laid out formula: its glyphs, drawn like typeset text, for instance with org.openrndr.extra.textwriter.SlugTextDrawer.addGlyphs, and its rules, the bars of fractions and the overbars of radicals, which are filled rectangles. Its baseline starts at origin; it is width wide and reaches ascent above and descent below the baseline.

layoutMath

fun layoutMath(formula: MathNode, face: Face, table: MathTable? = null, origin: Vector2 = Vector2.ZERO, display: Boolean = false, style: TextStyle = TextStyle.Defaults, shaper: TextShapingDriver = TextShapingDriver.instance): MathLayout

Lays out formula with face, a face of a math font, with the start of its baseline at origin. The layout follows the OpenType MATH table of the face, table, which the text shaping driver gives when it is null. A MathNode.Formula says itself whether it is displayed; display is for other nodes. The glyphs have style, like its fill, with the face and the size of the formula.

fun parseMathML(source: String): MathNode

Reads a formula in presentation MathML, like <math><mfrac><mi>a</mi><mn>2</mn></mfrac></math>. Elements that are not known are read as rows of their children. Character references and the common named entities are replaced.
