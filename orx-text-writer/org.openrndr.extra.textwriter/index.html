@JvmName(name = "typesetTextInShapes")
fun typesetText(spans: List<TextSpan>, shapes: List<Shape>, style: TextStyle = TextStyle.Defaults, shaper: TextShapingDriver = TextShapingDriver.instance): List<TypesetLine>

Typesets spans into shapes using Knuth-Plass line breaking. Every shape is filled from top to bottom with lines spaced by the line height, a line is placed where the full height of the line (from ascent to descent of the face of the first span) fits inside the shape. Where a shape is interrupted, by a hole or a concave part, a single baseline can hold multiple lines, these are filled from left to right, or from right to left for right-to-left paragraphs. Parts narrower than the line height are skipped. Text flows from one shape to the next, text that does not fit in any of the shapes is discarded.
