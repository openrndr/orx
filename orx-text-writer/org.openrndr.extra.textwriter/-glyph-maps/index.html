GlyphMaps

object GlyphMaps(source)

The glyph maps of boundary drawers, one for every boundary drawer: everything that draws text with the same boundary drawer, like compositions and user interfaces, finds the outlines of glyphs in the same map, and uploads every glyph once.

Glyphs whose outlines keep changing, those of variable faces whose axes are animated, would make that map grow without end, since every master has outlines of its own. Their glyphs go into a transient map instead, one per boundary drawer as well, which is cleared as a whole once it uses more than transientBudget bytes. A face goes there when it is marked with setTransient, also a face that is not variable, or by itself when its font is seen with more than churnMasters masters within churnSeconds. Who keeps indices of the transient map, like SlugTextDrawer, finds them again when its BoundaryGlyphMap.generation changed.
