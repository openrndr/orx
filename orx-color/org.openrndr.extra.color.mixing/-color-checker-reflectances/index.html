ColorCheckerReflectances

object ColorCheckerReflectances(source)

Measured reflectance spectra of the 24 patches of the X-Rite (formerly Macbeth) ColorChecker Classic, for use with mixReflectance and reflectanceToRGBa.

Every spectrum has 38 samples from 380 to 750 nm in steps of 10 nm, the grid used by linearToReflectance and reflectanceToXYZ. The data was measured by N. Ohta, The basis of color reproduction engineering (1997), and is identical to the data in ISO 17321-1. It was taken from colour-science (https://github.com/colour-science/colour, BSD-3-Clause), resampled from 5 nm to 10 nm by taking every other sample. Every property returns a new copy of the spectrum.
