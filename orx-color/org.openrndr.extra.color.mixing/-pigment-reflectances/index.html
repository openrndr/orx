PigmentReflectances

object PigmentReflectances(source)

Measured reflectance spectra of historical and modern pigments, for use with mixReflectance and reflectanceToRGBa.

Every spectrum has 38 samples from 380 to 750 nm in steps of 10 nm, the grid used by linearToReflectance and reflectanceToXYZ. The spectra were measured with fiber optics reflectance spectroscopy (FORS) by Cultural Heritage Science Open Source (CHSOS, https://chsopensource.org) on the swatches of their Pigments Checker, see A. Cosentino, FORS spectral database of historical pigments in different binders, e-conservation Journal 2, 57-68 (2014). Each sample is the average of all measurements within 5 nm of its wavelength.

The swatches are pigment samples in a binder for pigment identification, not opaque paint layers, so mixing them with Kubelka-Munk theory is an approximation, in particular for transparent pigments such as lakes. Every property returns a new copy of the spectrum.
