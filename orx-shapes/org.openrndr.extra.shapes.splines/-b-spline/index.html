BSpline

class BSpline<T : LinearType<T>>(val controlPoints: List<T>, val degree: Int, val knots: List<Double>, val closed: Boolean = false)(source)

A B-spline curve of degree p with controlPoints and a non-decreasing knots vector of length controlPoints.size + p + 1, after Piegl and Tiller, "The NURBS Book" (Springer, 1997). The curve is defined on the domain from knot p to knot controlPoints.size, is C^(p-k) where a knot has multiplicity k, and stays inside the convex hull of every p + 1 consecutive control points.

Create one with BSpline.clamped, BSpline.periodic or by interpolation with interpolatingBSpline.

closed

fun insertKnot(u: Double): BSpline<T>

The spline with knot u inserted once, by Boehm's algorithm (Piegl and Tiller, algorithm A5.1); it describes the same curve. With k the last knot index at or before u and s the multiplicity of u, the control points k - p + 1 to k - s are replaced by points on the edges of the control polygon.
