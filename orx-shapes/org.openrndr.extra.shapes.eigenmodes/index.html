extractBoundaryEigenModes

fun RectifiedContour.extractBoundaryEigenModes(count: Int, resolution: Int = 128, random: Random = Random(0)): List<BoundaryEigenMode>

Extracts the count lowest-frequency eigenmodes of this rectified contour's outline, ignoring its interior entirely -- the boundary-only counterpart to Shape.extractEigenModes. resolution parameters equidistant in rectified (arc-length) space are treated as a cycle graph, and its graph-Laplacian eigenvectors extracted; see org.openrndr.extra.math.eigenmodes.extractCycleEigenModes. A RectifiedContour is required rather than a plain ShapeContour specifically so that equal steps in t are equal steps in arc length -- true by construction here, since ts[i] = i / resolution in rectified space -- which is what the cycle graph's uniform edge weights assume.
