BezierVolume

class BezierVolume(val points: List<List<List<Vector3>>>)(source)

A tricubic Bezier volume: a 4x4x4 lattice of control points, indexed as points[w][v][u], that maps the unit cube of u, v and w to a deformed block of space. Moving its control points bends, twists or tapers whatever is placed in it, see org.openrndr.extra.shapes.distort.envelope.
