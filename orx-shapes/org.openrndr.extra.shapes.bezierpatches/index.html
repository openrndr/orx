BezierVolume
Link copied to clipboard
class BezierVolume(val points: List<List<List<Vector3>>>)

A tricubic Bezier volume: a 4x4x4 lattice of control points, indexed as points[w][v][u], that maps the unit cube of u, v and w to a deformed block of space. Moving its control points bends, twists or tapers whatever is placed in it, see org.openrndr.extra.shapes.distort.envelope.

bezierVolumeDeformFunctionGlsl
Link copied to clipboard
const val bezierVolumeDeformFunctionGlsl: String

GLSL that deforms a vertex in a BezierVolume given as arguments, so that a shader can deform by several volumes: void bezierVolumeDeform(inout vec3 position, inout vec3 normal, vec3 points[64], vec3 corner, vec3 size) places position, in the coordinates of the basis box with corner and size, at the point of the volume at its u, v, w in the box, and maps normal by the inverse transpose of the Jacobian of that mapping, normalized. points are the 64 control points in the order points[w][v][u], at index w * 16 + v * 4 + u.

bezierVolumeDeformGlsl
Link copied to clipboard
const val bezierVolumeDeformGlsl: String

GLSL that deforms a vertex in a BezierVolume, the GPU form of org.openrndr.extra.shapes.distort.envelope for meshes: bezierVolumeDeformFunctionGlsl, and void bezierVolumeDeform(inout vec3 position, inout vec3 normal), which deforms by the volume that setBezierVolume sets without a prefix: the uniforms p_bezierVolumePoints, p_bezierVolumeCorner and p_bezierVolumeSize. Put it in the vertex preamble of a shade style and call it in its vertex transform, before anything that uses x_position and x_normal, as bezierVolumeShadeStyle does. For several volumes in one shader, use bezierVolumeDeformFunctionGlsl with prefixes.

bezierVolumeShadeStyle
Link copied to clipboard
fun bezierVolumeShadeStyle(basis: Box, volume: BezierVolume): ShadeStyle

A shade style that deforms meshes in volume by their places in the basis box, on the GPU, positions and normals alike; see bezierVolumeDeformGlsl. Instanced meshes are deformed in their own coordinates, before their instance transforms. Update it with setBezierVolume when the volume changes, and combine it with a shade style that colors the mesh, like with +.

fun BezierPatch.distort(shapeContour: ShapeContour, referenceRectangle: Rectangle = shapeContour.bounds, distanceTolerance: Double = 0.5): ShapeContour

Distort a shape contour: the image of shapeContour under the patch, with the referenceRectangle mapped to the unit square of u and v. Every segment is replaced by cubic pieces through the images of its points, with the directions there turned by the patch, halved until they are within distanceTolerance of the image.

fun BezierPatch3D.evenGrid(columns: Int, rows: Int): List<List<Vector2>>

Places on a grid over this patch whose lines are spaced evenly along the surface rather than in u, v, as rows of u, v: rows rows from v = 0 to v = 1, spaced by length along the patch where u is 0.5, and in every row columns places from u = 0 to u = 1, spaced by length along that row. Both counts are at least 2, so that the grid reaches the edges.

scatter
Link copied to clipboard
fun BezierPatch3D.scatter(distance: Double, random: Random = Random.Default, maxCount: Int = Int.MAX_VALUE, misses: Int = 3000, resolution: Int = 64): List<Vector2>

Places spread evenly over this patch, as u, v: places at least distance apart on the surface, by the distance in space between their points, until no more fit (a Poisson disk sampling). Candidates are drawn in proportion to the area of the surface, so where the patch stretches it gets as many places as it has room for, and where it is squeezed it gets few, unlike places spread evenly in u, v. Use frames to place things at them.

setBezierVolume
Link copied to clipboard
fun ShadeStyle.setBezierVolume(basis: Box, volume: BezierVolume, prefix: String = "bezierVolume")

Sets the uniforms of a volume on this shade style: the control points of volume and the corner and size of the basis box, as p_${prefix}Points (64 vec3, points[w][v][u] at index w * 16 + v * 4 + u), p_${prefix}Corner and p_${prefix}Size. The default prefix gives the uniforms that the two-argument bezierVolumeDeform of bezierVolumeDeformGlsl reads; other prefixes are for bezierVolumeDeformFunctionGlsl, one per volume. Call it again when the volume changes.
