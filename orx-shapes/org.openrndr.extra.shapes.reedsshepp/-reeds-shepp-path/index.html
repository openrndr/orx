ReedsSheppPath

class ReedsSheppPath(val start: Vector2, val startDirection: Vector2, val radius: Double, val segments: List<ReedsSheppSegment>)(source)

A Reeds-Shepp path: a path from start with the car heading in startDirection, for a car that drives forwards and in reverse and turns at most as sharply as a circle of radius. It consists of up to five segments; where the sign of their lengths changes, the car changes gear and the path has a cusp.
