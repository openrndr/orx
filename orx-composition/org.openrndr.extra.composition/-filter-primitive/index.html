FilterPrimitive

sealed class FilterPrimitive(source)

A primitive of a FilterEffect: it computes an image from its inputs, which is the input of the next primitive and, when it has a result name, of later primitives that refer to it. Lengths are in the units of the FilterEffect.primitiveUnits.
