hitTest
Link copied to clipboard
fun Composition.hitTest(point: Vector2): List<CompositionHit>

The nodes at point, topmost first, like SVG hit-testing: shapes are hit on their fill and stroke as their pointer-events allow, with their fill rule; text on the cells of its glyphs, images on their rectangle. Nodes that are not displayed, and points outside of clip paths, are not hit. Groups are not hit themselves, the nodes they contain are; CompositionNode.parent leads to them. The point is in the coordinates the composition is drawn in, those of the drawer before the composition is drawn.
