UnicodeBidi

enum UnicodeBidi : AttributeOrPropertyValue, Enum<UnicodeBidi> (source)

How the direction of text is applied in bidirectional text, like the unicode-bidi property: NORMAL follows the bidirectional algorithm, EMBED and ISOLATE embed the text in the direction of its element, the overrides set every character in that direction, PLAINTEXT takes the direction from the text
